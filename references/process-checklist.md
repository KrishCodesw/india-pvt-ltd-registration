# Full incorporation sequence (for the checklist output)

1. Choose structure & gather KYC — PAN, Aadhaar, photo, and address proof (<2 months old) for every director/subscriber.
2. Get a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) for every director, from a licensed certifying agency.
3. Part A of SPICe+ — reserve the company name on MCA V3 (up to 2 options, with rationale).
4. Part B of SPICe+ — file director details, DIN allotment, capital, and registered office on MCA V3.
5. e-MOA (INC-33), e-AOA (INC-34), and AGILE-PRO-S (GST/EPFO/ESIC/bank account/Professional Tax) filed alongside Part B.
6. A practicing CA/CS/CMA digitally certifies and signs Part B — this is a legal requirement, not optional paperwork.
7. Pay the fees (based on the estimate this skill produces) and submit for ROC review.
8. Receive CIN, PAN, TAN, and the Certificate of Incorporation together; then open the company's bank account and apply for GST/licences as needed.

When rendering this as a checklist to the user, mark items 1–5 as complete only when the corresponding conversation section has actually been filled in — don't mark ahead of what's genuinely been gathered.

# CA handoff packet — section outline

When generating the `.docx` packet (after reading `/mnt/skills/public/docx/SKILL.md`), structure it in this order so a professional can scan it in one pass:

1. **Header** — "Incorporation prep packet — prepared by [founder's name(s)] for review", with today's date. Note clearly it's founder-prepared, not filed or verified.
2. **Business** — activity description, and the activity category if the founder gave one.
3. **Directors** — one line per director: name, PAN, resident/non-resident, DIN status.
4. **Capital** — authorised, issued, face value per share, and the resulting share count.
5. **Proposed names** — both options in order of preference, with their stated rationale, and the "provisional — not yet checked on MCA" caveat.
6. **Registered office** — address, proof type, and proof date.
7. **Estimated statutory fees** — the same breakdown shown in conversation, with the same exclusions noted (professional fees, stamp duty, GST/licences).

Keep the packet factual and neutral — it's a briefing document for the CA, not a pitch or a marketing piece.
